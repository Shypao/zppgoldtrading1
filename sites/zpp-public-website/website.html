04 / Checking the metal

What XRF tells us.

XRF stands for X-ray fluorescence. The analyzer reads the elements present in the area being tested.

We explain the reading alongside the item’s weight and condition. A surface reading is not a guarantee of the composition of the whole item.

Before you come in

Does the estimate guarantee an offer?

No. The calculator uses the published rate and the weight you enter. The shop checks your item’s actual purity, net weight, and condition before making an offer.

My grade isn’t listed on the flyer. Can I bring it?

The flyer lists 10K, 14K, 18K, 21K, 22K, and 24K. Message the shop to confirm other grades and the requirements for papel de ahensya.

Can I ask about testing before I visit?

Yes. Read the XRF explanation and message the shop about your item and testing requirements.
