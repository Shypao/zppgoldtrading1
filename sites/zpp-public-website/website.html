Plain-language guide

How does XRF
testing work?

XRF stands for X-ray fluorescence. It’s a technique that helps identify which elements are in a sample. We’ll help you understand what it measures for your item.

XRF measures the elements it can detect in the area that is tested. It doesn’t tell us everything about an item, so ask us what it can and can’t show for yours.

What is XRF testing?

A way to identify which elements are in a sample using X-rays. It’s non-destructive and gives a measured composition for the tested area.
